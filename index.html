My story

From Edmonton to Vancouver

Two cities, one sport I love, and a lot of time spent creating. These are the moments that made me who I am.

  1. The start

    Born in Edmonton, Alberta

    I was born in Edmonton, a city where winter lasts a long time and hockey is part of everyday life. Outdoor rinks, cold mornings and hockey on TV are part of what Edmonton is known for, and that's where my story begins.

  2. Age 4

    Moved to Vancouver

    When I was four, my family moved across the Rockies to Vancouver. Everything was new: a new city, new friends, the ocean and the mountains. Vancouver has been home ever since, but I never lost my love of winter and the ice.

  3. On the ice

    Joined the St. George's School hockey team

    Hockey is my main sport, and making the St. George's School team was a big moment for me. Playing on a team taught me to work hard in practice, stay focused when a game gets tough, and play for the people beside me. I play lots of other sports too, because I just like competing and staying active.

  4. Behind the screen

    Started editing videos

    Video editing started as a hobby and turned into something I really care about. I like finding the best moments in a clip, cutting them to the right beat, and adding effects that make people want to watch again. Sports gave me a lot to edit, and editing helped me see the game in a new way.

  5. Now

    Editing, building, creating

    Today I'm still playing hockey, still editing, and always trying something new. I use AI to design and build websites like this one, and I build worlds in Minecraft. What I'm aiming for is always the same: make something people will remember.
